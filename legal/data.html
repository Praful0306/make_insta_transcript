Your Data: Download and Delete

You control your data. Sign in on the Library page:

  • Download my data gives you a file with your account, subscription, API key list (never the keys themselves) and usage.
  • Delete my account removes your account, API keys and usage history from our database and your sign-in from Firebase. It cannot be undone. Cancel your plan first if you do not want to lose paid days; deleting does not by itself refund a payment.

Free-mode transcripts and rules are in your own Neon database. You can export or delete them there at any time; we do not hold a copy.
