Reel Script Desk

Paste Instagram reel links, separated by commas. Each reel is transcribed with Whisper large-v3, translated to English when it's Hindi, Kannada or any other language, and listed in the same order you pasted.

Three reels run at a time, about 30–60 seconds each, so 30 reels take roughly 8–12 minutes. Keep this page open until it finishes. Only public reels work.

What you'll get for each reel
  1. The reel's cover image and account, so you can check it's the post you meant.
  2. A short title and the topic.
  3. Editing notes: hook, structure, pacing and style.
  4. The full script in English, never shortened. For non-English reels the original-language transcript is shown too.
